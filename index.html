How it protects your voice

Microphone audio is processed inside this browser, replayed once, then discarded. It is never sent to a server. Safari will ask before using your microphone. You can stop access in Safari settings. The app itself does not save your recordings, use cookies or send analytics. Your web host may still receive ordinary website requests when the page loads.
